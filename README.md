# MSAB100
## Microsoft Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect AB-100 Professional Certificate
### Professional Certificate - 6 course series

The Microsoft Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Professional Certificate prepares you to design, build, & govern production-ready agentic AI solutions across the Microsoft AI platform. You'll learn to architect multi-agent systems, configure intelligent automation, and deliver enterprise AI solutions meeting world standards for performance, security, and compliance.

Across five long courses, you'll develop judgment in platform strategy, solution design, agent development & extensibility, monitoring and operations, Copilot Studio and Microsoft 365 Copilot architecture, and Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and application lifecycle management (ALM). You'll work through scenarios across financial services, insurance, retail, manufacturing, and customer experience—applying decision frameworks to select platforms, design agent topologies, model ROI, and operationalize AI at scale.

By the end of this program, you'll be equipped to lead agentic AI architecture conversations, evaluate trade-offs across the Microsoft AI ecosystem, & deliver solutions meeting enterprise-grade governance, safety, and operational requirements. Select courses align to the Microsoft AB-100 exam.
